# Project Specification

**Project:** Supply Chain Analytics & Control Tower  
**Fictional Company:** PackRight Manufacturing

## Purpose
Unify orders, production, inventory, suppliers, and logistics into a business-facing operational control tower.

## Domains
1. Demand
2. Inventory
3. Suppliers
4. Orders
5. Production
6. Logistics
7. Forecasting
8. Exceptions

## Entities
Customer, Product, Supplier, Facility, Carrier, Sales Order, Production Order, Inventory Snapshot, Purchase Order, Supplier Delivery, Shipment, Forecast, Supply Chain Exception.

## Minimum history
18 months.

## Main output
Power BI-ready control tower supported by a Python/SQL pipeline and FastAPI.

All data is synthetic.
